# Unemployment Analysis in India (CodeAlpha Internship - Task 2)

Analysis of unemployment rate data in India (May 2019 - June 2020), with a focus on the impact of Covid-19.

## What I did
- Cleaned the data (removed empty rows, fixed column names and dates)
- Plotted the unemployment trend over time
- Compared Rural vs Urban unemployment
- Found the states most affected during the lockdown
- Checked for seasonal patterns before Covid

## Key findings
- Unemployment rose from about 10% to about 24-25% in April-May 2020 and recovered to about 12% by June 2020
- Urban areas were hit harder than rural areas
- Puducherry, Jharkhand, Tamil Nadu and Bihar saw the sharpest rise (in this dataset)
- No clear seasonal pattern before Covid (data covers only about 10 normal months)
